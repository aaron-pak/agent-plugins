## Computer use

For tasks in apps on my Mac, use Cua: the `cua-driver` skill and its `mcp__cua-computer-use__*` tools, not the Claude app's own computer-use tools. Taking over my screen is fine while you do a task I asked for: when background input doesn't work, switch to foreground input (`delivery_mode:"foreground"`, `foreground_fallback`, or desktop input) without asking first.
